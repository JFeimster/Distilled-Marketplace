# Provider source context

This note preserves background about the sources used in the Marketplace taxonomy. It is not an approval checklist or publication gate. The owner has approved the 154 v3 candidates for directory listing; link health, current partner attribution and optional content are tracked separately from publication.

## Source boundaries

- Owner-saved referral URLs establish the destination supplied for the Marketplace CTA, not that current attribution or a partner account is active.
- Official provider sites can support product descriptions and pricing when the corresponding facts and sources are present.
- Public partner-program pages describe a provider's program, not a contract with Moonshine Capital.
- Historical articles, network records and campaign observations remain historical evidence and must not be described as current membership or customer pricing.
- Category labels and cross-property handoffs describe discovery architecture, not provider endorsement.

The v3 source inventory and provenance remain under `_reference/provider-intake-v3/`. The importer projects basic owner-approved listings into `data/marketplace/catalog.json`, strips private commercial metadata, and preserves supplied dates and unknowns. Enrichment can continue without withholding an otherwise valid directory listing.
